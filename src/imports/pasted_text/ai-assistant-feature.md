## ADD THIS NEW FEATURE TO THE EXISTING PLATFORM: AI ASSISTANT

Do NOT redesign or replace the existing website.

Keep all existing patient, caregiver, and doctor interfaces, games, Dear Diary, dashboards, navigation, colors, components, and functionality.

Add a new **AI Assistant feature** across all three interfaces.

The AI should feel like a friendly conversational assistant similar to ChatGPT/Meta AI, but specifically designed for this dementia-care platform.

---

# 🤖 1. AI ASSISTANT FOR PATIENT

Add a prominent **AI Companion / Talk to Me** option on the patient home screen.

The patient should be able to interact with the AI in TWO ways:

### 🎤 Voice

The patient can tap a large microphone button and speak naturally.

Show different visual states:

* Idle
* Listening
* Processing
* AI speaking

When the patient speaks, the AI should respond **with voice AND display the response as text**.

Example:

Patient:

> "I want to play a memory game."

AI:

> "Of course 😊 Let's play together."

Then automatically open the Memory Game.

### ⌨️ Typing

Add a large text input option.

If the patient types a message, the AI should respond **in text**.

Example:

Patient:

> "I want to listen to music."

AI:

> "Of course! What would you like to listen to?"

Then provide options such as:

**Assamese Music**

**Manipuri Music**

**Khasi Music**

**Regional Classics**

---

# 🧭 2. AI MUST BE ABLE TO NAVIGATE THE APP

This is a major feature.

The patient should NOT have to manually search through menus.

They can simply tell the AI what they want.

Examples:

> "Open my diary."

→ Open Dear Diary.

> "I want to play a game."

→ Open Games.

> "Play some Bihu music."

→ Open Music and show Bihu content.

> "Show me my medicines."

→ Open Medication.

> "Show my old memories."

→ Open Diary History.

> "I want to talk to my caregiver."

→ Open caregiver communication.

> "Take me home."

→ Return to Patient Home.

The prototype should visually demonstrate these navigation flows.

The AI should feel like an **assistant controlling the application**, not just a chatbot.

---

# ❤️ 3. PATIENT AI PERSONALITY

The AI should be:

* Sweet
* Patient
* Warm
* Calm
* Friendly
* Respectful
* Encouraging
* Non-judgmental

It should be especially suitable for elderly dementia patients.

Use short, simple sentences.

The AI should never make the patient feel bad for forgetting something or asking the same question repeatedly.

Avoid:

❌ "You already asked that."

❌ "That's incorrect."

❌ "You failed."

Instead use:

✅ "That's okay ❤️"

✅ "Let's try together."

✅ "Take your time."

✅ "Would you like me to help?"

The AI should feel like a **friendly companion**, not a medical chatbot.

---

# 🌐 4. MULTILINGUAL AI

Connect the AI with the existing language system.

The AI should support the application's existing languages, including:

* Assamese
* Manipuri
* Khasi
* Bengali
* Nagamese
* English
* Hindi

The AI should communicate in the patient's selected language.

If the patient speaks in their native language, respond in that language.

If the patient switches languages, allow the AI to switch naturally.

Include a visible language/voice setting.

---

# 🎭 5. AI PERSONALITY / VOICE CUSTOMIZATION

Add a simple setting:

### "Choose your AI companion"

Options:

👩 Female

👨 Male

🧑 Neutral

The selection should change the AI avatar/persona and voice representation.

Also provide simple controls for:

* Voice speed
* Voice volume
* Language

Do not make these settings complicated.

---

# 💬 6. AI CHAT INTERFACE

Create a dedicated conversational screen that matches the existing design system.

Include:

* AI avatar
* Large readable chat bubbles
* Microphone button
* Text input
* Send button
* Voice/text toggle
* Listening animation
* Speaking animation

Add simple suggested actions for elderly patients:

**🎮 Play a game**

**📔 Open my diary**

**🎵 Play music**

**💊 My medicines**

**💬 Talk to my caregiver**

**❤️ Talk to me**

These should be clickable.

---

# 📔 7. AI + DEAR DIARY INTEGRATION

The AI should work together with the existing Dear Diary feature.

Example:

Patient:

> "I want to tell you about my childhood."

AI:

> "I'd love to hear about it ❤️. Would you like to record it in your diary?"

Buttons:

**Yes, record it**

**Not now**

If the patient chooses Yes:

→ Automatically open Dear Diary recording.

Another example:

Patient talks about a memory.

AI:

> "That sounds like a lovely memory. Would you like to save it?"

→ Save to Dear Diary.

The AI should make the transition between conversation and diary feel natural.

---

# 👩‍👧 8. AI FOR CAREGIVERS

Add a separate AI Assistant to the existing caregiver interface.

Call it something like:

**🤖 Care Assistant**

The caregiver can type or speak questions.

Examples:

> "How was Mom today?"

> "What games did she play this week?"

> "Has her activity decreased?"

> "Summarize her diary entries."

> "Were any medicines missed?"

> "What has her mood been like recently?"

> "What did she eat today?"

The AI should provide concise summaries using the information already available in the caregiver dashboard.

Include buttons that allow the caregiver to jump to the relevant section:

**View Activity**

**View Diary**

**View Medication**

**View Diet**

---

# 👨‍⚕️ 9. AI FOR DOCTORS

Add a separate AI Assistant to the existing doctor interface.

Call it:

**🤖 Clinical Assistant**

The doctor can type or speak questions.

Examples:

> "Give me a summary of this patient."

> "How has their engagement changed?"

> "Summarize the last 30 days of diary entries."

> "Show medication adherence."

> "Summarize nutritional concerns."

> "What emotional trends appear in the diary?"

> "Generate a patient progress summary."

The AI should present information clearly using the existing patient data.

Include buttons:

**View Supporting Data**

**View Mood**

**View Diary**

**View Medication**

**View Nutrition**

---

# 🩺 10. AI MEDICAL SAFETY

The AI should NOT appear to replace a doctor.

Do not design it as independently diagnosing patients or prescribing medication.

For doctor/caregiver insights, display a small label such as:

**AI-assisted insight**

and where appropriate:

**Requires professional review**

The AI should assist with understanding and organizing information.

---

# 🌏 11. NER PERSONALIZATION

The AI should work naturally with the platform's existing NER customization.

It can understand and discuss:

* Regional music
* Food
* Festivals
* Household objects
* Farming
* Family memories
* Local languages
* Cultural activities

For example:

Patient:

> "Play something from Assam."

AI:

> "Of course 😊. Let's listen to some Assamese music."

→ Open Music.

Do not stereotype patients based on their state.

Use the patient's selected language, region, interests and preferences for personalization.

---

# 🔗 12. AI SHOULD BE AVAILABLE THROUGHOUT THE APP

Do not make AI accessible only from one isolated page.

Add an AI entry point to the existing navigation/home interfaces:

### Patient

**🤖 Talk to Me**

### Caregiver

**🤖 Care Assistant**

### Doctor

**🤖 Clinical Assistant**

Use a floating button, navigation item, or another unobtrusive method that matches the existing design.

Do not let it cover important controls.

---

# ⭐ MOST IMPORTANT UX REQUIREMENT

The AI should feel like:

> **"Someone who is happy to listen and help."**

Especially for the patient interface.

It should help the patient:

**Talk → Understand → Navigate → Play → Remember → Connect**

The AI is not just a chatbot.

It is an **intelligent companion and navigation assistant integrated into the existing application**.

Keep the existing website exactly as it is and **add this AI system into it without removing or redesigning the existing features.**
